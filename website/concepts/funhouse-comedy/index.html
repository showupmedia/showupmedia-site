About Funhouse

Founded by comedian Spiky Mike

Funhouse Comedy was founded by Nottingham comedian Spiky Mike, and our award-winning shows are renowned for very friendly audiences and a wonderful atmosphere. Acts to have played our clubs since we launched in 2003 include Sarah Millican, Rhod Gilbert, Romesh Ranganathan, Josh Widdicombe, Zoe Lyons, Scott Bennett, Josh Pugh and Gary Delaney.

As well as booking pro acts, we are always looking for total beginners for our friendly “Should I Stay or Should I Go?” gong shows – and Spiky Mike is at every one.
