Since 2003

Founded by Nottingham comedian Spiky Mike. Friendly rooms, top acts.

Read more

Funhouse Comedy was founded by Nottingham comedian Spiky Mike, and its award-winning shows are known for very friendly audiences and a great atmosphere. Acts to have played the clubs since 2003 include Sarah Millican, Rhod Gilbert, Romesh Ranganathan, Josh Widdicombe, Zoe Lyons, Scott Bennett, Josh Pugh and Gary Delaney.

As well as booking pro acts, Funhouse runs friendly “Should I Stay or Should I Go?” gong shows for total beginners, and Spiky Mike is at every one.
