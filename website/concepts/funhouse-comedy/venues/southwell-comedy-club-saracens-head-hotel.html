Southwell Comedy Club – Saracens Head Hotel

Southwell Comedy Club by Funhouse takes place on the second Thursday of each month, as well as occasional extra shows, at The Saracen’s Head Hotel. Having previously featured acts including Hal Cruttenden, Joe Lycett and Gary Delaney, it is regarded by many top acts as one of the best comedy club nights in the country.

Good to know

  • Funhouse comedy club nights have been running at this venue and its predecessor, The Admiral Rodney, since 2014
  • Many comedy club shows sell out in advance, so early booking is strongly advised
