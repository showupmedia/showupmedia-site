FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does a clipping agency cost?

Our Clips Starter plan is $150 a month for 30 clips (about $5 a clip). Long-form editing is £99–150 per episode depending on length. Every new creator can get 3 free clips first.

What do I need to send you?

Just a link to your podcast, YouTube, Twitch or Kick channel. We pull public episodes and VODs ourselves. For private files, share a download link such as Google Drive or Dropbox.

How do you pick the moments?

We read the full transcript of every episode (and chat activity for streams), then a human editor picks and cuts the best moments. No random auto-clips.

Do you edit Twitch streams and podcasts as well as YouTube videos?

Yes. We clip Twitch and Kick streams, video podcasts, IRL vlogs and YouTube videos, and we also edit full long-form episodes for YouTube. See our services.

Can you match my existing style?

Yes. Send us a few clips you like and we will match the fonts, colours, caption style and layout, or design a new look with you.

How long are the clips?

Mostly around 30 seconds, which is best for reach and retention. For creators monetising on TikTok and Facebook we also cut 60–90 second versions.

Where are you based?

Show Up Media is a UK clipping and video editing agency, working with creators in the UK, US, Australia and Asia.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are monthly and you can cancel anytime. Start with 3 free clips and decide after.
