Legal

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Show Up Media is a UK-based clipping and video editing agency. This page explains, in plain English, what information we hold about you, why we hold it and what you can ask us to do with it. If anything is unclear, email admin@showupmedia.org.

Who we are

Show Up Media ("we", "us") is the data controller for the information described here. We are based in the United Kingdom. You can reach us at admin@showupmedia.org.

What we hold

  • Contact details: your name, email address and channel or social links, from our free-sample form, emails or direct messages.
  • Content you share: videos, audio, episode links and any other files you send us so we can edit them, plus the clips and edits we make from them.
  • Payment records: invoices and records of payments made through PayPal or by bank transfer to our Tide business account. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Messages: emails and messages about your work, feedback and approvals.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you and send your free sample.
  • To deliver the editing work you ask for, and to post it to your platforms when you ask us to.
  • To send invoices, take payment and keep accounts.
  • To keep a record of what we agreed and what we delivered.

Our legal reasons are: taking steps to enter, and then carry out, a contract with you; our legal duties (for example tax records); and our legitimate interest in running and improving our service. We will only send you marketing if you have agreed to it or are already a client, and you can opt out at any time.

How long we keep it

  • Content files (your raw footage and our edits): for as long as we work together, then deleted within 90 days of the work ending unless you ask us to keep them.
  • Contact details and messages: for as long as we work together and up to 2 years after, so we can answer questions about past work.
  • Invoices and payment records: 6 years, as UK tax law requires.
  • Free-sample requests that do not become clients: up to 12 months.

You can ask us to delete your data sooner, subject to what the law requires us to keep.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We only share it with the services we need to run the business:

  • Proton: our email and encrypted file storage.
  • PayPal and Tide: to process and record payments.
  • Netlify: hosts this website and receives free-sample form submissions.
  • Social and video platforms (such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X): only when you ask us to upload or schedule posts for you.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use the safeguards UK law requires, such as approved contract clauses. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Get a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your data.
  • Object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it.
  • Ask for your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email admin@showupmedia.org. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Keeping it safe

We store files and email with encrypted services, limit access to the people doing your work, and delete files we no longer need.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Questions? Email admin@showupmedia.org.
